Screen access

This copy of Rox is running as a web app. Android screen access needs the Rox Android app (Accessibility service), so the switch will stay off until you use that version.

Rox will read your screen and tap or type only when you ask it to. It never works inside payment or banking apps, never reads passwords or OTPs, and you can turn this off anytime.

Android does not let apps turn this on silently, so this one-time step is needed.

1. Open Accessibility settings.
2. Tap Rox Assistant.
3. Turn it on.

While this is on, Google Pay, PhonePe and other UPI apps may block payments. Close Rox or turn the switches off before paying.
